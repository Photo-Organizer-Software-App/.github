## 🚀 Introduction
Most photo libraries grow faster than anyone can manage them by hand. Photo Organizer for Windows steps in as digital photo organizer software that reads the information stored in each image, sorts everything by date and place, and gives your collection a structure you can actually navigate. It tags pictures to make them findable and roots out duplicate copies that quietly eat up storage. Whether your photos live in a single overflowing folder or spread across your whole drive, this software brings them together into one coherent, searchable library that stays right where it belongs — on your PC.

## ✨ Highlights

### Organize and clean up
- **Sort by date and location** — Automatically groups images by capture time and place for a structure that makes sense at a glance.
- **Find and clear duplicates** — Detects identical and near-identical shots so you can free up space with confidence.
- **Keeps your library local** — Works on the files already on your machine and organizes them in place.

### Find and enjoy

## 💻 System Specs
**Minimum**
- OS: Windows 10 64-bit
- Processor: A modern 64-bit processor
- Memory: 4 GB RAM
- Storage: Room for the app plus your photo library and its index

**Recommended**
- OS: Windows 11 64-bit
- Processor: A recent multi-core 64-bit processor
- Memory: 8 GB RAM or more
- Storage: An SSD with generous free space for large libraries
